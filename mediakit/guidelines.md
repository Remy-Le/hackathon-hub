### Choosing the Right Version (Brandmark VS. full Logo)

Use the **brandmark** when space is limited or the Hackathon Hub brand is already clear from context.

Use the **full logo** when introducing the brand, working with external partners or creating official communication materials.

## Brandmark (Find versions below)

Use the standalone brandmark for compact applications such as profile pictures, favicons, social graphics or small digital placements.

<aside>
💡

**Do**

- Use the black version on light backgrounds.
- Use the white version on dark backgrounds.
- Keep sufficient clear space around the brandmark.
- Scale it proportionally.
- Use the official supplied files.
</aside>

<aside>
💡

**Don’t**

- Stretch, rotate or distort the brandmark.

- Change its colours.
- Add shadows, outlines or other effects.
- Place it on visually busy or low-contrast backgrounds.
- Recreate or modify its shape.
</aside>

## Full Logo (Find versions below)

Use the full logo whenever the Hackathon Hub name needs to be clearly communicated, particularly in presentations, partner materials, press publications and event communications.

<aside>
💡

**Do**

- Use the black version on light backgrounds.
- Use the white version on dark backgrounds.
- Keep the brandmark and wordmark together as supplied.
- Maintain clear space around the entire logo.
- Ensure the wordmark remains readable at the chosen size.
</aside>

<aside>
💡

**Don’t**

- Separate or rearrange the brandmark and wordmark.
- Change the font, spacing or proportions.
- Place additional text inside the logo’s clear space.
- Use the full logo at a size where the wordmark becomes difficult to read.
- Place the logo over images without sufficient contrast.
</aside>
