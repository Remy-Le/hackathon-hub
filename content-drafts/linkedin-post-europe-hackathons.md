# LinkedIn/social — Hackathons as a travel hack for meeting locals

You won't make a local friend on a walking tour. You will on a hackathon team.

You spend a weekend with strangers, solving a problem together under a deadline. That's a different kind of connection than small talk in a hostel common room.

A few things that make it work:

- You're placed with locals immediately. No small talk phase, you're already working with them by hour one.
- Most run 24-48 hours with food and a venue included. A cheap way to spend a weekend in a new city.
- The people you meet are usually students or engineers who actually live there. They'll tell you where to eat, what's overpriced, and who's hiring.
- The connections last past the weekend. Hackathon group chats and alumni networks keep going long after the event ends.

Check Hackathon Hub for what's running in the city you're headed to next.
