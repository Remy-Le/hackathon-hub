# LinkedIn/social — Hackathons as a way in at startups

Startups get 500 applications for one role. They remember the person who built something in front of them.

If you want to stand out to a startup, join a hackathon they're at.

1. You show your actual skills: writing code, debugging under pressure, making decisions in real time. No resume bullet does that.
2. You meet the team in person. You pick up things nobody in that 500-person application list will ever know: how they actually work, what they're building next, who makes the calls.
3. Startups sponsor hackathons to scout talent. Showing up puts you in the room they're already recruiting from, before the job posting even goes up.

Find startup-backed hackathons on Hackathon Hub.
