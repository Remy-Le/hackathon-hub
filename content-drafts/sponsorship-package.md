# Hackathon Hub — Sponsorship Package

*Draft v1 — 2026-08-23. Pricing and terms below are a first proposal, not yet confirmed live anywhere public. Review before sending to a real prospect. Voice matches the rest of the B2B content: founder-led, transparent about Phase 1, no corporate sales-speak.*

## 1. One-Pager (send this first, before a call)

**Hackathon Hub is Europe's central platform for discovering hackathons, competitions, and challenges** — 615+ events, 33+ countries, a community-verified leaderboard, and a 90+ person ambassador network sourcing participants and sponsors on the ground in 25+ countries.

**Why sponsor here instead of one event directly:**
- You're not buying a logo on one banner for one weekend. You're getting a standing presence across a pan-European pipeline of events, refreshed continuously — not a one-off spend.
- The ambassador network is a sourcing layer, not a passive directory. It's people actively connecting sponsors to the right regional communities, not just distributing your logo to whoever registers.
- The leaderboard gives you a signal generic sponsorship doesn't: which participants have an actual track record across multiple events, not just who clicked "register" once.

**Where we are, plainly:** Hackathon Hub is Phase 1 — currently free to use, donation-supported, no live monetization yet. This sponsorship program is the first paid offering we're introducing. We don't have case studies or past sponsor logos to show you yet. What we do have is real infrastructure (615+ events, 90+ ambassadors, an active leaderboard) and founding-sponsor terms that reward being early.

**What you get, in one line per tier:** see Section 2.

**The ask:** a sponsorship agreement, invoiced directly, paid by bank transfer — no self-serve checkout yet, this is still a direct, founder-led relationship.

## 4. Organizer Offering (separate from sponsors — stays free)

Organizers are **not** part of this pricing sheet. Submitting and listing an event stays free, per the existing Phase 1 / community-first positioning — do not conflate this with sponsor pricing in outreach.

What organizers get, for reference (already drafted in `content-drafts-b2b-segments.md`):
- Free event submission, automatic categorization, pan-European distribution.
- Access to a participant pool with leaderboard track records (helps address no-show rates).
- Optional: organizers can be introduced to sponsors interested in their specific event/region, once sponsor demand exists — this is the natural bridge between the two sides of the marketplace, worth mentioning to sponsors as a differentiator ("we can connect you to organizers directly, not just list your logo").

## 5. Talent / Recruiting Offer (Talent Partner tier detail)

This is the most differentiated, highest-price offer, and the one most worth over-explaining in a proposal — it directly answers the sponsor fear "90% of sponsors never meet a future employee on-site."

**What's included at Talent Partner level:**
- **Leaderboard-based candidate visibility** — view top-ranked participants (by the independent, cross-organizer leaderboard) filtered by country, skill theme, or event type.
- **Direct intro to top performers** — the team facilitates an introduction to a shortlist of leaderboard-ranked hackers who've opted in to being contacted by sponsors (opt-in only — never sell contact access to hackers who haven't agreed to it).
- **Ambassador-sourced candidate pipeline** — ambassadors in relevant countries can proactively flag strong participants matching a sponsor's hiring profile.
- **Priority access to upcoming "elite" events / investor mixers** — early visibility into which participants are being invited to higher-tier events, a signal of track record beyond a single hackathon.

**Positioning line for outreach:** *"You're not sponsoring a booth. You're getting a sourcing layer into the European hackathon talent pool that's still sorted by actual track record, not just who registered."*

**Honesty check for this section:** the leaderboard/opt-in candidate pool is still early — do not promise a guaranteed number of candidate intros or hires. Frame it as access to infrastructure, not a filled recruiting funnel, consistent with the Phase 1 "we're not hiding where we are" stance used elsewhere.

## 9. Payment Model

- **No self-serve checkout.** Every sponsorship is arranged directly with the founder, invoiced manually.
- **Payment method:** bank transfer against an invoice. (PayPal donation link stays separate — it's for the existing donation flow, not sponsorship payments, to avoid mixing up accounting.)
- **Terms:** invoice sent on agreement, covering the full term upfront (not billed monthly), payment due within 14 days, assets go live once payment is confirmed (not on invoice send).
- **Term length:** rates are quoted per month, but the minimum commitment is 3 months, invoiced as one lump sum for the term; renewal is a manual re-invoice, not auto-billed (no infrastructure for that yet).
- **Refunds:** no refund policy exists yet — if this comes up before a real sponsor, treat it as a founder decision, not something to promise proactively in outreach.

---

## 10. Discount & Approval Rules

- **Standing rule:** the first 10 sponsors ever get 20% off their first term, on any tier. This is the only discount that should be quoted without checking with the founder first.
- **Tracking:** keep a running count of founding-sponsor slots used (out of 10) — once full, stop offering it and revert to list price.
- **Anything outside this rule** (multi-event bundles, a discount beyond 20%, a custom tier, a longer/shorter term, in-kind trade instead of cash) needs founder approval before it's offered to a prospect — there's no self-serve authority for non-standard deals yet.
- **Do not** discount the Talent Partner tier further to "make the recruiting angle land" — if a recruiting-focused prospect balks at price, that's a signal to have the founder join the call, not to unilaterally cut the price.
