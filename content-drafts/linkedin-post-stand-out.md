# LinkedIn — Standing out in the job market (hackathon CTA)

500 applicants, 1 interview. That's the real math right now.

Everyone has a degree now. Practice is what makes you stand out.

A few ways to build it while you're still in school:

- Internships. They show you can work inside a real team, on someone else's timeline.
- A part-time job that builds the actual skill you're going for. Bartending won't get you a software engineering role.
- Side projects. Pick one you can actually finish and point to, not a half-built app that never shipped.
- Freelancing. Real clients and real deadlines, not a grade at the end.
- Hackathons. Short sprints, low cost to experiment. GroupMe started as a hackathon project and sold to Skype for $80M less than a year later.

Hackathons compress the most learning into the least time out of all five. A weekend gets you a shipped project, a team you didn't know before, and a deadline that forces you to decide fast instead of overthink.
