## 1. Channel assignment by awareness level (Participants)

| Level | Primary channel | Format | Secondary channel |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Unaware | Instagram/TikTok Reels, LinkedIn (founder personal) | Short narrative video or single-image story post — the "3am version of you" angle | Twitter/X thread |
| 2. Problem Aware | Blog (site), LinkedIn | Long-form personal essay (the drafts already written) | Repurposed as a LinkedIn native post + Twitter/X thread |
| 3. Solution Aware | Blog (SEO-targeted), Twitter/X | Comparison-style long-form ("Devpost vs us") | LinkedIn carousel summarizing the comparison |
| 4. Product Aware | Landing page, email (existing users), blog | Proof-point page section + dedicated blog post | Retargeting ad copy pulled from the same proof points |
| 5. Most Aware | Email, push notification, in-app | Short templated, dynamically personalized (leaderboard rank, new-this-month digest) | None — this tier shouldn't be static blog content |

## 2. Channel assignment by segment (B2B)

| Segment | Primary channel | Format | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Organizers | LinkedIn, direct email to submitted-event contacts, blog | Long-form + a one-page "why submit" PDF/landing page | CTA: submission flow |
| Sponsors | LinkedIn (founder + company page), 1:1 outreach, blog | Long-form case-for-sponsorship post + a simple one-pager (no pricing yet — lead with transparency) | Needs sales-enablement follow-up once pricing exists |
| Ambassadors | Instagram, Discord/community channels, referral from existing ambassadors | Recruiting-style post + a short recorded testimonial from a current ambassador (not yet drafted — needs real quotes) | Cheapest channel: word-of-mouth inside the existing 90+ network |

## 4. Seasonality hook (the piece still missing)

This plan assumes a steady cadence, but the content itself should cluster around real hackathon-calendar moments:
- **Application/recap season spikes** — most European university hackathons run in two windows (roughly Oct-Dec and Feb-May). Content volume (especially Level 1 "why you should care" pieces) should be heavier just before those windows open, not evenly distributed year-round.
- **Recap-driven FOMO content** (Level 1, piece #1's exact angle) works best posted within days of a major hackathon wrapping, riding the actual recap posts circulating on LinkedIn/Discord — this needs a lightweight "watch for wrapped events" process, not a pre-scheduled calendar slot.
- This calendar doesn't yet have that event-calendar overlay. Recommend building a simple list of 10-15 known recurring European hackathons with dates, and slotting reactive content around them.
